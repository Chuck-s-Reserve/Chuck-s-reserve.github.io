The Reading Room

Books for Growing Together

A little practice can change how we meet the day. A shared idea can change how we meet each other. These books by Steven Gray belong beside the work we do at Chuck’s Reserve: learning, rebuilding, and making room for a more giving world.

Daily Practice

Two Sides to Me: My Personal Neuro Strengthening Workbook

By Steven C. Gray

A neuro workbook for strengthening both brain hemispheres through ambidexterity practice — using both hands to learn in a new way. It trains the mind the way the store trains community: with practice, patience, and room to grow. Take it one exercise at a time, at your own pace.

The Bigger Picture

Liberty to Unity

By Steven C. Gray · America’s Silent Arc Toward Voluntary Global Anarchy

Humanity’s change from eat-or-be-eaten toward unity is slow, but every act of cooperation matters. This book traces that journey and explores why a transparent, gift-based economy is the natural next step — a world where we freely give and receive. It’s the bigger hope behind the small acts of service we practice on Main Street.

An Open Invitation

Bring Your Curiosity

Read a little, ask a question, or share what giving and receiving look like in your life. Our faith points us toward heaven on earth through kindness and cooperation. You’re welcome in the conversation, and just as welcome to browse quietly. Faith is offered, never required.

Thanksgiving gratitude stays with us, and so does Christmas — in July and every day of the year. Life is ever giving!
