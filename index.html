Neighbors Helping Neighbors

A Little Shop, a More Giving World

Our Main Street Mission

Chuck’s Reserve is a 501(c)(3) thrift store on Historic Main Street in Garden Grove, built on neighbors helping neighbors. A donated jacket, a kind welcome, a skill shared across the shop floor — small acts of care help us rebuild together. We practice transparency, service, and faith in the everyday work of giving and receiving.

We believe humanity is growing into something kinder. We remember where we’ve been, from taking to sharing and from competing to cooperating, so we never forget why neighbors helping neighbors matters. That change can reach the last corners of the world, one neighbor at a time.

Giving Is the Bigger Picture

Our hope is a gift-based resource economy: a world where people freely give what they can and receive what they need. We see that as heaven on earth, as prophesied — a future where care and cooperation make government and world currency unnecessary. Here on Main Street, we start with what we can share today.

Local donations keep the racks and shelves changing. Come browse, bring something useful, or share a little of your time. Cash and Zelle are welcome, and there’s a dressing room for trying things on.

A Little Reading, a Bigger Conversation

Two Sides to Me and Liberty to Unity, books by Steven Gray, carry this same hope into daily practice and the bigger story of humanity. Curious? Visit our Books page and take a look.

A Grateful Season

This Thanksgiving, we’re grateful for our Historic Main Street neighbors, the folks who donate, and everyone who stops in to browse, gather, and give things a second life. Your everyday kindness is what keeps this little shop giving.

Christmas All Year

We celebrate Christmas in July and basically every day of the year! Ornaments, décor, and holiday pieces are ready for their next celebration. Pink is our color of purity, gold brings a little sparkle, and the joy is in sharing. Life is ever giving!

The Floor Plan

How the Shop Works

The store is arranged so daytime browsing and evening gatherings can share the same room — books up front, vintage sound in the middle, and Star Goat Studios in the back third.

Front — Books & Rolling Racks

The front of the shop is home to our books. Clothing racks are moving onto new casters so they can roll aside in the evening and open the floor for small groups, up to the number the city permits.

Middle — Retro Sound

The middle of the store is dedicated to retro VHS, cassette tapes, and magnetic reel tape. Those titles are available for purchase. Once we have city permission, we also plan to rent the equipment so the tapes and reels can actually get used.

Back — Star Goat Studios

Clothing hangs along the back walls of the rear third of the shop, where it also helps quiet the room. That back third will be home to Star Goat Studios: a soundproof music recording studio, and right beside it a stage with stage lighting, cameras, and a green screen. The stage is part of our confidence-building classes, helping people get comfortable on camera.

Here for the Community

Clothing When You Need It

If you are in need, you may receive a set of clothing once per week. Come in during open hours and ask — we are glad to help.

A Place to Stay Engaged

Chuck’s Reserve partners with local workforce and community programs to bring neighbors onto the sales floor — greeting guests, folding, and helping people find what they need. It’s a place to stay engaged, learn retail skills, and rebuild together. Healing is part of what we hope this store brings to the world, through kindness, service, and a place to belong. If you’d like to lend a hand or learn alongside us, come say hello.

Love & Ceremony

The Ministry for Eros Officiant

Faith is part of the heart of this shop. Our ministry is led by an ordained minister, and sharing the good word with neighbors who choose to engage is a joy. Faith is offered, never required — you’re welcome to shop, learn, and lend a hand just as you are.

Eros Officiant is our wedding and ceremonial officiant ministry. If you’re planning a ceremony or would like a conversation, visit Eros Officiant or ask at the shop.
